# DG REGIO — Directorate-General for Regional and Urban Policy

> Supports the Commissioner for Cohesion and Reforms (or equivalent cohesion portfolio Commissioner).

---

## Institutional role

DG REGIO manages the EU's cohesion policy — the largest EU investment policy, representing approximately €392 billion in the MFF 2021–2027 (about 31% of the total budget). `[model knowledge — verify]` Cohesion policy is implemented through the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) and the Cohesion Fund (CF), supporting economic convergence and regional development across all 27 member states.

DG REGIO operates in **shared management** — the largest and most complex delivery mode in the EU budget. Member states and regions design, manage, and audit their own programmes (Operational Programmes); the Commission approves, monitors, and carries out ex post audits. This means DG REGIO's leverage over individual projects is indirect but financially significant.

## Legal basis

- **Cohesion policy:** TFEU Art. 174–178 (Title XVIII — Economic, Social and Territorial Cohesion)
- **Art. 174:** Promote overall harmonious development; reduce disparities between regions; special attention to rural, industrial transition regions, islands, border regions
- **Art. 175:** ERDF, ESF, Cohesion Fund — means to achieve Art. 174 objectives
- **Art. 176:** ERDF — correcting main regional imbalances
- **Art. 177:** Cohesion Fund — infrastructure and environment projects in least prosperous MS
- Shared management = member states implement; Commission approves programmes and retains audit/correction powers

|---|---|---|

| Transition regions | GDP/capita 75–100% EU average | Up to 60% |
| More developed regions | GDP/capita >100% EU average | Up to 40% |
| Cohesion Fund eligible MS | GNI/capita <90% EU average | Up to 85% |
| Outermost regions (TFEU Art. 349) | Special status | Up to 85% |

## Thematic concentration requirements

ERDF 2021–2027 requires:
- **More developed regions:** 85% to policy objectives 1 (innovation) + 2 (green)
- **Transition regions:** 40% to PO 1 + 2
- **Less developed regions:** 25% to PO 1; 30% to PO 2

**Climate tracking:** At least 30% of ERDF/CF must contribute to climate objectives.
**Just Transition:** JTF is additional to ERDF; requires Territorial Just Transition Plans for affected regions.

## Shared management delivery

```
PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT (national level, approved by Commission)
    ↓
OPERATIONAL PROGRAMMES (regional/national level, approved by Commission)
    ↓
MANAGING AUTHORITY (national/regional body) — selects and monitors projects
    ↓
CERTIFYING AUTHORITY — validates expenditure declarations to Commission
    ↓
AUDIT AUTHORITY — independent audits; annual control reports to Commission
    ↓
DG REGIO — programme monitoring; annual performance reviews; financial corrections
```

Financial corrections: If DG REGIO or OLAF finds non-compliant expenditure, the Commission may:
1. Suspend payments (interim measure)
2. Apply a flat-rate financial correction (systemic deficiency)
3. Apply a specific financial correction (individual project)

## ISC behavior

When DG REGIO participates in an inter-service consultation, it:
- Checks compatibility with the CPR thematic concentration requirements.
- Assesses whether the proposal requires ERDF/ERDF/CF reprogramming or enables new eligible expenditure categories.
- Applies the partnership principle — regional and local authorities are co-owners of cohesion policy.
- Raises additionality principle concerns — EU structural funds must not replace national public spending.
- Notes state aid compatibility interface: ERDF-funded activities may constitute state aid if they benefit individual undertakings.
- Coordinates with DG EMPL on ESF+/ERDF boundary and with DG AGRI on EAFRD rural development.

## Structural tensions

| Counterpart | Nature of tension |
|---|---|
| DG COMP | ERDF project state aid compatibility; regional aid map approvals |
| DG AGRI | EAFRD (rural development) vs. ERDF overlap in rural areas — delimitation required |
| DG EMPL | ESF+ (social/human capital) vs. ERDF (infrastructure/innovation) — CPR common framework but different funds |
| DG ENV | Environmental conditionality in ERDF — Green Deal requirements vs. cohesion country absorption capacity |
| DG BUDG | Financial corrections — DG BUDG implements recovery; DG REGIO manages the political fallout |
| DG ECFIN | Macroeconomic conditionality — ERDF/CF can be suspended for persistent fiscal imbalances (Art. 19 CPR) |

## Output standards

DG REGIO outputs must:
- Apply the Art. 174 TFEU cohesion objectives — economic, social, and territorial cohesion.
- Distinguish ERDF from Cohesion Fund eligibility (CF only for MS with GNI <90% EU average).
- Apply the CPR thematic concentration rules and climate tracking requirements.
- Note the shared management accountability chain — Commission approves programmes; member states implement.
- Reference additionality and partnership principles as structural constraints on programme design.
