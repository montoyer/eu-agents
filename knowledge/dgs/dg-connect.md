# DG CNECT — Directorate-General for Communications Networks, Content and Technology

> Supports the Executive Vice-President for Digital Transformation (or equivalent digital portfolio Commissioner).

---

## Institutional role

DG CNECT is the EU's digital policy lead. It shapes legislation and policy across the full digital economy: digital infrastructure (connectivity, 5G, spectrum), digital platforms (DMA, DSA), artificial intelligence, data economy, cybersecurity, and digital public services. It is the driving force behind the EU's Digital Decade strategy.

DG CNECT operates at the intersection of industrial policy (connectivity investment, data economy) and regulation (platform rules, AI safety, cybersecurity obligations). It coordinates closely with DG COMP on ex post competition enforcement and with DG GROW on product safety/standardisation aspects of digital products.

## DMA gatekeeper framework

The Digital Markets Act designates large online platforms as "gatekeepers" when they:
- Provide a "core platform service" (search, OS, browser, social network, messaging, video sharing, virtual assistant, cloud, ads)
- Meet quantitative thresholds (turnover ≥€7.5bn or market cap ≥€75bn; ≥45m monthly EU end users; ≥10,000 business users) `[model knowledge — verify]`
- Have an "entrenched and durable" market position

Designated gatekeepers must comply with Art. 5 (per se obligations — no negotiation) and Art. 6 (contestability obligations — implementation details can be specified by the Commission). DG CNECT leads gatekeeper proceedings; DG COMP retains Art. 101/102 jurisdiction (parallel regimes).

## AI Act risk-based framework

| Risk tier | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Unacceptable risk (prohibited) | Real-time remote biometric surveillance in public spaces (with exceptions), social scoring, manipulation |
| High risk | Conformity assessment, technical documentation, human oversight, registration in EU database |
| Limited risk | Transparency obligations (e.g., chatbots must declare AI nature) |
| Minimal risk | No mandatory requirements |

GPAI (General Purpose AI) models: specific obligations for foundation models; enhanced obligations for systemic-risk models (≥10^25 FLOPs training compute). DG CNECT leads AI Office (integrated in Commission structure).

## ISC behavior

When DG CNECT participates in an inter-service consultation, it:
- Checks whether the proposal affects digital infrastructure, platform services, or AI systems regulated under the DMA/DSA/AI Act.
- Applies the Digital Decade KPI framework (2030 targets: 1 Gbps coverage, 5G, digital skills, public services).
- Flags ex ante/ex post jurisdictional boundaries with DG COMP (DMA vs. antitrust).
- Checks interoperability and data portability requirements under the Data Act/DGA.
- Raises cybersecurity risk implications (NIS2 obligations, CRA product requirements).
- Notes audiovisual/media pluralism concerns for content-related proposals.

## Output standards

DG CNECT outputs must:
- Distinguish DMA (gatekeepers, ex ante), DSA (platform liability, ex ante/post), and antitrust (Art. 101/102, ex post) — these are separate regimes with different thresholds, procedures, and standards.
- Apply the AI Act risk-tier classification to any AI system under consideration.
- Note NIS2 essential entity/important entity classification and relevant incident-reporting obligations.
- Reference the Digital Decade 2030 targets when assessing connectivity and digital skills dimensions.
