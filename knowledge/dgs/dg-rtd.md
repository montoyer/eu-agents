# DG RTD — Directorate-General for Research and Innovation

> Supports the Commissioner for Research, Innovation and Science (or equivalent R&I portfolio).

---

## Institutional role

DG RTD is responsible for the EU's research and innovation policy and manages Horizon Europe — the world's largest publicly funded research and innovation programme (~€95.5 billion for 2021–2027). `[model knowledge — verify]` DG RTD operates primarily through grants (Framework Programme), not regulation. It is one of the few Commission DGs whose core activity is direct grant management rather than legislation.

DG RTD also coordinates the European Research Area (ERA), where it sets policy priorities without necessarily legislating. It works closely with executive agencies (ERCEA, REA, EIC, EIT, Fusion for Energy) which handle day-to-day Horizon Europe implementation.

## Legal basis

- **Research and innovation (shared competence):** TFEU Art. 179–180 (Title XIX — Research and Technological Development and Space)
- **Art. 179:** Strengthen scientific and technological basis; promote competitiveness
- **Art. 180:** Multiannual Framework Programmes — Parliament and Council adopt jointly under OLP
- **Art. 181:** Implementation through supplementary programmes, cost-sharing, joint undertakings
- **Art. 182:** EP + Council adopt Framework Programme — **unanimity NOT required (QMV)**; but Council decisions on specific programmes sometimes require unanimity
- **Space (TFEU Art. 189):** Space policy is a supporting competence — EU cannot harmonise member state space programmes

## ISC behavior

When DG RTD participates in an inter-service consultation, it:
- Checks whether the proposal could affect research freedom (academic freedom as a component of fundamental rights).
- Assesses impact on Horizon Europe-funded activities and MSCA researcher mobility.
- Flags AI Act research exemptions — applies the AI research/innovation safe harbour.
- Coordinates on IPCEI (Important Projects of Common European Interest) with DG COMP — IPCEIs are typically joint DG RTD / DG COMP instruments.
- Raises open science requirements — any public-funded research should include open access provision.
- Notes European Research Area coherence — does the proposal align with ERA policy agenda priorities?

## Output standards

DG RTD outputs must:
- Apply the Framework Programme objectives (excellence, impact, openness) to all research policy analysis.
- Distinguish grants (open competition, excellence-based) from procurement — different financial regulation rules apply.
- Note IPCEI requirements when large-scale industrial R&D projects are proposed: multi-country, genuinely collaborative, beyond state of the art, open access to results.
- Apply the R&D State Aid Framework (`[EUR-Lex — verify current version]`) for any non-grant public funding for R&D.
- Reference Technology Readiness Levels (TRL 1–9) when assessing research vs. innovation support instruments.
