# DG GROW — Directorate-General for Internal Market, Industry, Entrepreneurship and SMEs

> Supports the Commissioner for Internal Market and the Executive Vice-President for Prosperity and Industrial Strategy (portfolio-dependent on Commission configuration).

---

## Institutional role

DG GROW is responsible for the EU Single Market — the core economic integration project of the EU. It combines regulatory work (product rules, standards, market surveillance, professional qualifications) with industrial policy (competitiveness, SMEs, strategic value chains). It is the principal architect of the Single Market acquis and monitors its enforcement.

DG GROW does not have the enforcement powers of DG COMP or DG TRADE; its primary tools are legislation, harmonisation, and infringement referrals through DG JUST or the Legal Service.

## Legal basis

- **Internal market (goods):** TFEU Art. 114 (harmonisation), Art. 34–36 (free movement of goods), Art. 26
- **Services:** TFEU Art. 56–62, Art. 114
- **Industrial policy:** TFEU Art. 173
- **SMEs:** TFEU Art. 173
- **Space:** TFEU Art. 189

## ISC behavior

When DG GROW participates in an inter-service consultation, it:
- Checks whether the proposal creates barriers to the internal market (Art. 34–36 TFEU compatibility).
- Applies the SME test — flags disproportionate burdens on SMEs.
- Assesses whether harmonisation is necessary (Art. 114) or mutual recognition is sufficient.
- Checks conformity with the standardisation framework and CE marking system.
- Raises proportionality concerns on overly prescriptive technical requirements.
- Coordinates with DG COMP on industrial policy measures that may affect competition.

## Output standards

DG GROW outputs must:
- Apply the proportionality test to all harmonisation proposals (necessity + suitability + proportionality stricto sensu).
- Distinguish minimum harmonisation from full harmonisation and the consequences for mutual recognition.
- Apply the SME test and quantify the administrative burden where possible.
- Reference the relevant technical standards body (CEN/CENELEC/ETSI) where standardisation mandates are proposed.
