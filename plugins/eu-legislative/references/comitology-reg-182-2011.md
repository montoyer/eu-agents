## Choosing the procedure (Art. 2, verified)

The basic act selects the procedure "taking into account the nature or the
impact of the implementing act required."

**Examination procedure applies in particular for:**
- (a) implementing acts of **general scope**;
- (b) other implementing acts relating to: (i) **programmes with substantial
  implications**; (ii) the **common agricultural and fisheries policies**;
  (iii) the **environment, security and safety, or protection of the health
  or safety of humans, animals or plants**; (iv) the **common commercial
  policy**; (v) **taxation**.

**Advisory procedure** applies as a general rule to everything else — and may
apply to the Art. 2(2) categories "in duly justified cases".

## Common provisions (Art. 3, verified)

- Committee of Member State representatives, **chaired by the Commission
  (chair does not vote)**.
- Meetings convened **not less than 14 days** from submission of the draft act
  and agenda (except duly justified cases); time limits for opinions must be
  proportionate and give members an effective opportunity to examine the draft.
- Until the opinion is delivered, **any member may suggest amendments** and
  the chair may table amended versions; the chair "shall endeavour to find
  solutions which command the widest possible support".
- **Written procedure** possible in duly justified cases; silence before the
  deadline = tacit agreement; terminated without result if the chair decides
  or **a member requests** (a meeting must then be convened).
- **Appeal committee**: meets at the earliest 14 days (except duly justified
  cases) and at the latest **6 weeks** after referral; delivers its opinion
  **within 2 months** of referral; chaired by the Commission.

## Advisory procedure (Art. 4, verified)

Opinion if necessary by vote, by **simple majority of component members**.
The Commission decides "taking the utmost account" of the conclusions and
opinion — it is not bound.

## Examination procedure (Art. 5, verified)

Opinion by the **qualified majority of Art. 16(4)–(5) TEU** (and Art. 238(3)
TFEU where applicable), votes weighted accordingly.

| Committee outcome | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Positive opinion** | Commission **shall adopt** — but per the annexed joint statement of the EP, Council and Commission (verified), the Commission may "in very exceptional cases" take into account **new circumstances arising after the vote** and decide not to adopt, after duly informing the committee and the legislator |
| **Negative opinion** | Commission **shall not adopt**. If an act is deemed necessary: amended version to the **same committee within 2 months**, or referral to the **appeal committee within 1 month** |
| **No opinion** | Commission **may adopt**, *except* where: (a) the act concerns **taxation, financial services, protection of the health or safety of humans, animals or plants, or definitive multilateral safeguard measures**; (b) the **basic act so provides** (no-opinion clause); or (c) **a simple majority of component members opposes** it. In those cases: amended version to the same committee within 2 months, or appeal committee within 1 month |

Art. 5(5) (verified): special track for **definitive anti-dumping /
countervailing measures** where no opinion + simple-majority opposition —
Member State consultations, then referral to the appeal committee on fixed
14-day/1-month timelines.

## Appeal committee (Art. 6, verified)

Same QMV rule. **Positive opinion → shall adopt; no opinion → may adopt;
negative opinion → shall not adopt.** Exception (Art. 6(4)): **definitive
multilateral safeguard measures require a positive opinion** — silence is not
enough.

## Exceptional and urgent adoption (Arts. 7–8, verified)

- **Art. 7**: notwithstanding a negative opinion or a blocking no-opinion, the
  Commission may adopt where needed "without delay" to avoid **significant
  disruption of agricultural markets** or a **risk for the Union's financial
  interests (Art. 325 TFEU)** — immediate referral to the appeal committee;
  if it opines negatively, the act is **repealed immediately**.
- **Art. 8 (immediately applicable acts)**: where the basic act so provides on
  "duly justified imperative grounds of urgency", the Commission may adopt
  **without prior committee submission**; the act remains in force **max 6
  months** (unless the basic act provides otherwise); it goes to the committee
  **within 14 days**; a negative examination-procedure opinion forces
  **immediate repeal**. Art. 8(5): provisional anti-dumping/countervailing
  measures use this route.

## Transparency and scrutiny (Arts. 9–11, verified)

- **Art. 9**: each committee adopts rules of procedure by simple majority,
  based on Commission standard rules published in the OJ.
- **Art. 10**: public **comitology register** — committee list, agendas,
  summary records, draft acts, voting results, final drafts, adoption
  information, statistics; EP and Council receive documents at the same time
  as committee members; annual report on committee work.
- **Art. 11 (right of scrutiny)**: for basic acts adopted under the **OLP**,
  the EP **or** the Council may **at any time** signal that a draft
  implementing act **exceeds the implementing powers** in the basic act; the
  Commission must review it and state whether it will **maintain, amend or
  withdraw** the draft.

## Legacy: 1999/468/EC and the RPS (Arts. 12–14, verified)

Decision 1999/468/EC is repealed, but the effects of its **Art. 5a (RPS) are
maintained** for existing basic acts that refer to it (Art. 12, recital 21).
Alignment of remaining RPS acts to delegated/implementing acts is ongoing
`[model knowledge — verify the current alignment status]`.

**Art. 13 transitional mapping** — when reading a pre-2011 basic act, its
references to Decision 1999/468/EC translate as follows (verified):

| Basic act refers to (1999/468/EC) | Read as (Reg. 182/2011) |
|---|---|
| Art. 3 (advisory) | Art. 4 advisory procedure |
| Art. 4 (management) | Art. 5 examination — **without** the no-opinion blockers of Art. 5(4) 2nd–3rd subparas |
| Art. 5 (regulatory) | Art. 5 examination — basic act **deemed to provide** that no opinion = no adoption (Art. 5(4)(b)) |
| Art. 6 (safeguard) | Art. 8 immediately applicable acts |
| Arts. 7–8 | Arts. 10–11 (register; right of scrutiny) |

Art. 7 (exceptional adoption) applies only to procedures formerly under
Art. 4 of 1999/468/EC; pending procedures where a committee had already
opined remain under the old decision (Art. 14, verified).
