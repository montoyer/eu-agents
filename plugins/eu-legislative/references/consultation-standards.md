## Verified deadlines and language rules

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Open public consultation** (Have Your Say portal) | **12 weeks**, launched after the related 'call for evidence' document |
| **Call for evidence** feedback period | **4 weeks** |
| Call for evidence document | Published on Have Your Say **in all EU languages**, written in plain language — short and clear |
| Combined call for evidence + consultation | Possible; the 12 weeks of public consultation must be preserved; questionnaire may be uploaded later only if the full 12 weeks remain |
| **Synopsis report** | A detailed, qualitative analysis of **all** consultation activities; the factual summary of OPC results must be published **within 8 weeks of closure** |

## The consultation strategy (Tool #52)

Defined at inception, discussed with the interservice coordination group:
- objectives and scope of each consultation activity;
- stakeholder mapping (categories, interest/influence/expertise, underserved
  groups — see `references/stakeholder-categories.md`);
- choice of methods per target group: open public consultation for the general
  public; targeted questionnaires, interviews, SME panels, workshops and
  hearings for specialised groups;
- sectoral social dialogue committees where relevant (Treaty-based social
  partner consultation is a distinct, mandatory track — Tool #10);
- languages and accessibility per activity.

## Conducting and analysing (Tools #53–#54)

- Questionnaires: structured so non-specialists can respond; neutral, no
  leading questions; every IA/evaluation option should be testable from the
  responses `[model knowledge — verify against Tool #53]`.
- Moderation rules: abusive, off-topic or campaign content may be excluded —
  document the criteria.
- Campaign responses (coordinated identical submissions) are identified and
  reported separately `[model knowledge — verify against Tool #54]`.
- The **synopsis report** explains how stakeholder input was (or was not) used
  in the initiative/evaluation, and mentions consultation activities outside
  the strategy for transparency.

## Horizontal matters (Tool #55)

Publication of responses on Have Your Say, personal-data protection
(EUDPR-compliant privacy statements), and access-to-documents handling of
submissions.
