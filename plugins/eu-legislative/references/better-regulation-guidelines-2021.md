## Mandatory requirements (verified highlights)

- DGs **must present the results of all impact assessments, evaluations and
  fitness checks in staff working documents (SWDs)**.
- Impacts on **SMEs must be screened and assessed**; territorial, rural and
  geopolitical dimensions transparently assessed.
- **Mandatory internet-based public consultation: minimum 12 weeks** (for
  initiatives subject to IAs and evaluations); revision consultations cover
  both the backward- and forward-looking dimensions in one consultation.
- The lead DG **must prepare a consultation strategy early** and include its
  key elements in the **call for evidence**.
- The **'evaluate first' principle** applies before revisions of EU
  legislation.

## RSB opinions (verified, Guidelines wording)

- **Impact assessments — three opinion types**: *positive* (proceed, taking
  account of recommendations), *positive with reservations* (proceed, but
  **must revise the IA report** accordingly), *negative* (the DG **has to
  submit an amended impact assessment to the Board**).
- **Evaluations and fitness checks — only positive and negative**; after a
  negative opinion the DG **has the option** to resubmit an amended report.
- **A positive RSB opinion is needed before inter-service consultation can be
  launched** for IA-supported initiatives (Commission working methods).
- RSB opinions are published in the Register of Commission Documents once the
  proposal is adopted.

For the second-negative-opinion derogation route (Commissioner responsible for
better regulation), see the Toolbox-based `references/rsb-criteria.md` — the
Guidelines and the December 2025 Toolbox phrase the follow-up differently;
both are quoted from source in the respective files.

## One In, One Out (verified)

- Estimates of **adjustment and administrative costs** are quantified in IAs
  where feasible and proportionate; **administrative burdens are subject to
  offsetting**, adjustment costs compensated "to the greatest extent
  possible".
- If no IA could be produced where one was warranted, the Commission presents
  **justified cost-benefit estimates in analytical reports alongside the
  proposal or within 3 months**.
- **Offsetting cascade** (verified): annual administrative burdens are
  reported in the dedicated online administrative burden calculator and offset
  within (1) the **remit of the DG** in question; failing that (2) the
  **REFIT area** directly affected; failing that, escalation per the
  Guidelines `[model knowledge — verify the final cascade step]`.

## Proportionate analysis

The depth of consultation, evaluation and IA work is scaled to the
significance of the initiative ("principle of proportionate analysis") — the
operational test is Toolbox Tool #12 (`better-regulation-toolbox-2025.md`).
