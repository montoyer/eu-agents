## Principle (verified)

EU actions and policies should pull together for a successful and just
transition in line with the **European Green Deal**; environmental aspects are
assessed **proportionately and following the 'do no significant harm'
principle**. Evaluations and IAs also systematically assess the relevance and
coherence of current legislation with these priorities.

## In consultations (verified)

Stakeholder consultations explicitly cover environmental aspects where
relevant — via the call for evidence, dedicated public-consultation questions,
or targeted consultations.

## In evaluations (verified)

All evaluations ask:
1. Are there identified issues that **significantly harm the environment**?
2. Is there any **incoherence with the European Climate Law**?
3. If so, how could they be removed or minimised?

Where potentially significant negative environmental effects are found but
data are insufficient to quantify them, the evaluation must state the
methodological limitation, present the adverse effects qualitatively, and
record the data gap in lessons learned.

## In impact assessments (verified)

- Be explicit about environmental impacts and the **trade-offs between the
  environment and other objectives**, clearly identified in both the report
  and the proposal.
- Options must be **consistent with the Green Deal's environmental
  objectives**. Under the **European Climate Law**, the Commission assesses
  the consistency of any draft measure or legislative proposal — including
  budgetary proposals — with the **climate-neutrality objective** (Art. 2(1))
  `[EUR-Lex — verify Regulation (EU) 2021/1119, Art. 6(4)]`.
- A statement of compliance of the legislative proposal with the DNSH
  principle is included **where relevant** (typically in the explanatory
  memorandum and the IA's environmental section).

## Relationship to other screens

DNSH complements, not replaces, the full environmental-impacts identification
of Tool #36 and the sustainability screening of Tools #18–#19 (identification
of impacts, SDGs). For quantification, use Chapter 8 methods —
`references/cba-methodology.md` (incl. Tool #66 Life cycle assessment).
