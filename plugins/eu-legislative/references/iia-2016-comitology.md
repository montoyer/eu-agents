## What the IIA is

The binding framework agreed by Parliament, Council and Commission covering
programming, better-regulation tools, choice of legal acts, delegated and
implementing acts, transparency and implementation. For drafting purposes the
core is the **delegated/implementing acts section and the annexed Common
Understanding on Delegated Acts** with its standard clauses (verified).

## Delegated acts commitments (verified)

- The Commission commits to **gathering all necessary expertise before
  adopting delegated acts, including through the consultation of Member
  States' experts and through public consultations**.
- **Equal access**: EP and Council **receive all documents at the same time
  as Member States' experts**; EP and Council experts **systematically have
  access** to Commission expert-group meetings preparing delegated acts.
- The Commission may be invited to EP/Council meetings for further exchanges
  on the preparation of delegated acts.
- The institutions agreed to negotiate **non-binding criteria for the
  application of Arts. 290 and 291 TFEU** (delineation delegated vs
  implementing) — delivered as the 2019 non-binding criteria
  `[EUR-Lex — verify the 2019 follow-up instrument]`.

## Register of delegated acts (verified)

The institutions committed to a **joint functional register of delegated
acts** (operational since late 2017), providing well-structured information
and **traceability of every stage in a delegated act's lifecycle** — use it
to track objection periods and expert-group stages.

## Common Understanding standard clauses (verified structure)

The annexed Common Understanding supplies the standard empowerment wording
used in basic acts:
- duration of the delegation (indeterminate or fixed period with tacit
  extension);
- **revocation** by EP or Council;
- **objection period**: the standard clause is 2 months, extendable by 2
  months on EP/Council initiative `[EUR-Lex — verify the clause text in the
  annex]`;
- urgency procedure (act enters into force immediately, subject to
  objection);
- consultation of Member States' experts ahead of adoption (with feedback on
  how their input was handled).

Use these clauses verbatim when drafting empowerments — deviation invites ISC
and legal-revision pushback; see also `references/joint-practical-guide.md`.

## Other IIA strands worth knowing (verified structure)

Annual and multiannual interinstitutional programming (joint declarations);
better-regulation commitments (IAs on substantial amendments by the
co-legislators — rarely done in practice); transparency of trilogues;
implementation and gold-plating monitoring by Member States.
