## Cost typology (Tool #56, verified)

- **Direct costs/benefits** — clearly linked to the intervention:
  - **direct compliance costs** (charges, substantive compliance,
    administrative burden);
  - **enforcement costs** — implementation, monitoring, inspection by
    authorities.
- **Indirect costs/benefits** — observed in related upstream/downstream
  markets, transmitted through changes in prices, availability or quality.

## Administrative costs — EU Standard Cost Model (Tool #58, verified)

Administrative costs are a **specific type of compliance cost** incurred by
enterprises, public authorities and citizens in meeting **administrative
obligations**: labelling, reporting, registration, provision of data,
monitoring and assessment needed to generate the information — whether
transferred to authorities/private parties or merely held available for
inspection. SCM formula: tariff (wage × time) × quantity (population ×
frequency) `[model knowledge — verify formula presentation against Tool #58]`.

## One In, One Out (Tool #59, verified number)

Cost estimates feed the OIOO approach: new administrative burdens on
businesses are offset by equivalent reductions in the same policy area —
quantify and state the offset in the IA.

## Cost-benefit analysis (Tool #63, verified)

- Monetise costs and benefits over the relevant **time horizon**; discount to
  present value; report **NPV** (and benefit-cost ratio).
- **Social discount factor: 3 % in real terms** (lowered from 4 % in the 2017
  edition). With 2 % annual inflation, a 5 % nominal rate corresponds to the
  3 % real rate.
- Both NPV and BCR are **highly sensitive to the discount rate** — run
  sensitivity analysis with lower and higher values around the central 3 %
  (Tools #64–#65).
- Monetary valuation carries technical uncertainty: valuation technique, time
  horizon, discount rate, aggregation rule — state assumptions.

## Choosing the method

| Method | Tool | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| CBA | #63 | Costs and benefits monetisable on both sides |
| Cost-effectiveness analysis | #57 | Benefits quantifiable but not monetisable (cost per unit of outcome) |
| Multi-criteria decision analysis | #62 | Mixed quantitative/qualitative evidence — compares options on effectiveness, efficiency, coherence and proportionality with explicit weighting (verified framing) |
| Standard Cost Model | #58 | Administrative burden |
| Simulation models (CGE, partial equilibrium, micro-simulation) | #61 | Economy-wide or sectoral effects — document model and assumptions |
| Baseline construction | #60 | Every IA — the no-EU-action scenario all options are compared against |
