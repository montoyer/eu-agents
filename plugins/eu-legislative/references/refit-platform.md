## REFIT (verified)

- Launched **2012** (Regulatory Fitness and Performance Programme) to step up
  simplification and burden reduction; successor to the 2007 Administrative
  Burden Reduction Programme.
- Today REFIT requires **all evaluations and all revisions to systematically
  consider simplification and burden reduction** — without undermining the
  policy objectives — including the potential of digital transformation and
  innovative practices.
- REFIT is delivered through the ordinary better-regulation tools and must be
  considered (verified list):
  1. during planning;
  2. by the interservice coordination groups for evaluations and IAs;
  3. in the consultation of citizens and stakeholders;
  4. in evaluation reports;
  5. in impact assessments;
  6. in the RSB's assessment;
  7. in explanatory memoranda accompanying legislative proposals.
- **Annex II of the Commission work programme** lists the most relevant REFIT
  initiatives.
- Burden offsetting is operationalised through **One In, One Out** — Tool #59,
  see `references/cba-methodology.md`.

## The Fit for Future Platform (verified)

A **high-level expert group** bringing together the **Member States, the
Committee of the Regions, the European Economic and Social Committee and
stakeholders**, collaborating with the **SME Envoy Network**; it builds on the
experience of the predecessor **REFIT Platform**. The platform adopts opinions
on simplification potential in existing EU law, which feed the Commission's
annual work programme `[model knowledge — verify mandate details against
Tool #2]`.
