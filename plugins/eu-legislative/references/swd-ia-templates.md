Curated static reference for the `eu-legislative` skills (`impact-assessment`,

## Form and length (verified)

- The IA report is a **staff working document (SWD)** that the College takes
  note of when deciding on the initiative; it is transmitted to the other
  institutions and made public.
- **Benchmark length: 40 pages** for the main part (excluding cover page and
  annexes), in **non-technical language for non-expert readers**.
- **Executive summary: 4–5 standard DGT pages**, a **separate SWD translated
  into all EU languages**, presenting problems, objectives, justification for
  EU action, options and the preferred option in plain, visually accessible
  form for an uninformed reader.
- Official templates for the report and executive summary are on **GoPro**
  (internal — consult there for the current layout).
- The report must systematically address: subsidiarity, proportionality,
  sustainability, environmental, social, economic (incl. SME), digital and
  fundamental-rights impacts, contributions to relevant **SDGs**, and who is
  affected and how.

## Mandatory annexes (verified)

| Annex | Content |
|---|---|
| **1 — Procedural information** | Lead DG, Decide/work-programme references; chronology, ISCG composition and number of meetings; **tabular account of how each RSB recommendation (opinion boxes B and C) changed the report** |
| **2 — Stakeholder consultation (synopsis report)** | All consultation activities, outcomes, and how input was used — see `references/consultation-standards.md` |
| **3 — Who is affected and how** | Practical implications per affected group; summary table of costs and benefits (broken down by main category); OIOO-relevant burdens on businesses and citizens singled out; measures addressing inequalities |
| **4 — Analytical methods** | Methods and models used, assumptions, limitations — see `references/cba-methodology.md` |
| **5 — Competitiveness check** | Compulsory; summarises the Tool #21 assessment |
| **6 — 'SME Check'** | For SME-'relevant' and 'highly relevant' initiatives — see `references/sme-test.md` |

## Drafting notes

- Prioritise information that helps the College weigh pros and cons of the
  options — the report is a decision document, not a research paper.
- Detailed analysis belongs in annexes; the main text carries the argument.
- The structure mirrors the IA analytical chain (problem → why EU →
  objectives → options → impacts → comparison → monitoring) — see
  `references/better-regulation-toolbox-2025.md`.
