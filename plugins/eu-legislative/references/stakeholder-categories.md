## Mapping method (verified key elements)

1. Identify **all stakeholder categories** relevant for or interested in the
   policy area.
2. Sort groups by **level of interest, influence and expertise** on the
   initiative.
3. Check whether a **sectoral social dialogue committee** or other sectoral
   committee is relevant to consult.
4. Do **not** stop at the obvious stakeholders — actively identify
   **underserved groups** at risk of exclusion; ISCG members contribute to
   this.
5. Data protection: reusing stakeholder lists from earlier consultations,
   networks, expert groups or the Transparency Register requires that the
   stakeholders opted in to be re-contacted for the same purpose.

## Stakeholder categories (verified, non-exhaustive)

| Category | Includes |
|---|---|
| Citizens | General public (non-expert individuals); individual expert citizens responding on their own behalf |
| Businesses | Large enterprises; SMEs; microenterprises; self-employed |
| Social partners and representatives of professions and crafts | Chambers of commerce; employers' organisations; business organisations; trade unions; representatives of professions or crafts |
| Non-governmental organisations | NGOs; platforms; networks; similar associations |
| Consultancy | Professional consultancies; law firms; self-employed consultants |
| Others `[model knowledge — verify remainder of Box 4 against Ch. 7]` | Academic/research institutions; public authorities (national, regional, local); EU agencies; international organisations; environmental/consumer organisations |

Organisations eligible for the **Transparency Register** that choose not to
register are treated as a separate category ('non-registered
organisations/businesses') unless recognised as representative via Treaty
provisions.

## Use in the consultation strategy

The mapping feeds the consultation strategy (Tool #52): choice of methods per
group (open public consultation reaches citizens; targeted questionnaires,
panels and workshops reach specialised groups), languages, and accessibility.
Evaluation consultations reuse the mapping after re-scoping. See
`references/consultation-standards.md`.
