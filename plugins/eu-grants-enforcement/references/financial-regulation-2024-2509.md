## Budgetary principles (verified list)

**Unity and budgetary accuracy · annuality · equilibrium · unit of account ·
universality · specification · sound financial management (economy,
efficiency, effectiveness) and transparency** — every implementing choice
(transfer, carry-over, assigned revenue) is an application of, or a derogation
from, one of these.

## Implementation methods (verified structure)

| Method | Who implements |
|---|---|
| **Direct management** | Commission departments, executive agencies |
| **Shared management** | Member States (cohesion, agriculture) — management & control systems, annual accounts, financial corrections |
| **Indirect management** | Third countries, international organisations, EIB/EIF, Union bodies, national agencies, etc., after pillar assessment |

## Financial actors and circuits (verified structure)

Authorising officers (by delegation/sub-delegation) — responsible for ex-ante
and ex-post controls and the **segregation of initiation and verification**;
accounting officer; internal auditor. Irregularity reporting and the **early
detection and exclusion system (EDES)** — exclusion grounds include grave
professional misconduct, fraud, corruption, significant deficiencies.

## Grants (verified structure — Title on grants)

- Core rules: annual work programmes and **calls for proposals**;
  **co-financing** and **no-profit** principles; **non-retroactivity**; award
  criteria published; evaluation by committee.
- Forms: reimbursement of eligible costs, **unit costs, lump sums, flat
  rates, financing not linked to costs** — simplified cost options are
  mainstream.
- Eligibility, single-submission, ex-post audit and recovery; financial
  corrections and the audit trail are the `grant-audit-advisor` core — keep
  amounts and time limits verified against the recast text
  `[EUR-Lex — verify the specific articles before citing numbers]`.

## Procurement (verified structure — Title on procurement + Annex I)

Procedures, thresholds aligned to the public-procurement directives,
exclusion/selection/award stages, standstill before signature, and the
negotiated procedures catalogue live in **Annex I** — operational figures in
`references/procurement-thresholds-2024.md`.

## Other instruments (verified structure)

Prizes, **financial instruments and budgetary guarantees**, contributions,
non-financial donations; trust funds; budget support to third countries.
