## Gatekeeper designation (Art. 3, verified figures)

An undertaking is designated a **gatekeeper** if it (1) has a significant
impact on the internal market, (2) provides a **core platform service (CPS)**
that is an important gateway for business users to reach end users, and
(3) enjoys an entrenched and durable position. Quantitative presumptions:

| Criterion | Threshold (verified) |
|---|---|
| Significant impact | Annual Union turnover ≥ **EUR 7,5 billion** in each of the last 3 financial years, **or** average market capitalisation ≥ **EUR 75 billion** in the last financial year, + CPS in ≥ 3 Member States |
| Important gateway | ≥ **45 million monthly active end users** established or located in the Union **and** ≥ **10 000 yearly active business users** established in the Union, in the last financial year |
| Entrenched and durable | The gateway thresholds met **in each of the last 3 financial years** |

Presumptions are rebuttable; the Commission can also designate below the
thresholds after a market investigation (Art. 17).

**Core platform services** (Art. 2(2), verified structure): online
intermediation, online search engines, social networking, video-sharing,
number-independent interpersonal communication services, operating systems,
web browsers, virtual assistants, cloud computing, online advertising
services.

## Obligations (Arts. 5–7, verified structure)

- **Art. 5** (self-executing): no combining/cross-using personal data across
  services without consent; no parity clauses preventing better offers
  elsewhere; no anti-steering restrictions; no forced single sign-in/identity
  service; no tying CPS to other CPS registration; advertising transparency.
- **Art. 6** (susceptible to specification): no self-preferencing in ranking;
  data access for business users; no use of non-public business-user data to
  compete with them; allow uninstalling/default changes and third-party app
  stores/sideloading; portability; FRAND access to app stores, search and
  social services; OS/virtual-assistant/browser choice screens.
- **Art. 7**: interoperability of number-independent interpersonal
  communications services with the gatekeeper's.

Compliance deadline: **6 months after designation** (Art. 3(10))
`[EUR-Lex — verify]`.

## Enforcement (verified figures)

| Tool | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fines (Art. 30) | Up to **10 % of total worldwide turnover**; up to **20 %** for repeated infringement of Arts. 5–7 |
| Procedural fines | Up to **1 % of total worldwide turnover** |
| Periodic penalties (Art. 31) | Up to 5 % of average daily worldwide turnover `[EUR-Lex — verify]` |
| Systematic non-compliance (Art. 18) | Market investigation; behavioural or **structural remedies**, incl. a ban on acquisitions in the relevant area |

**Merger interface**: gatekeepers must **inform the Commission of any
intended concentration** involving digital-sector or data-collection targets
(Art. 14) — regardless of EUMR thresholds; this also feeds possible Art. 22
EUMR referrals — see `references/merger-regulation.md`.

## Relationship to Art. 102 TFEU

The DMA applies **without prejudice** to Arts. 101–102; it is regulatory
ex-ante obligation, not a finding of dominance — a designated gatekeeper is
not thereby dominant, and Art. 102 cases may run in parallel.
