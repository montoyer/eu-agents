# Vertical Block Exemption Regulation — (EU) 2022/720

## Exemption and market-share thresholds (Arts. 2–3, verified)

Art. 101(1) TFEU is declared inapplicable to **vertical agreements** to the
extent they contain vertical restraints, provided:
- the **supplier's share ≤ 30 %** of the relevant selling market, **and**
- the **buyer's share ≤ 30 %** of the relevant purchasing market.

Special regimes (verified structure): **dual distribution** (supplier also
sells directly in competition with its distributors) remains covered at
wholesale/retail level, but **information exchange** between the parties is
exempt only where directly necessary to implement the agreement; **online
intermediation services** providers acting as hybrid platforms lose the
benefit for those services (Art. 2(6)).

## Hardcore restrictions (Art. 4 — benefit lost entirely, verified structure)

- **Resale price maintenance** — fixed or minimum resale prices (maximum and
  recommended prices allowed absent pressure/incentives).
- **Territorial/customer restrictions** on the buyer, subject to the named
  exceptions: exclusive distribution (restricting active sales into
  territories/customer groups exclusively allocated to ≤ 5 distributors),
  selective distribution (restricting sales to unauthorised distributors in
  the territory), wholesale/components carve-outs.
- **Online sales**: restrictions that, directly or indirectly, have the
  object of **preventing the effective use of the internet** by the buyer or
  its customers are hardcore; dual pricing and non-equivalent selective
  criteria for online sales are no longer hardcore per se (2022 change,
  verified theme).
- Restrictions on **cross-supplies within selective distribution** and on
  component suppliers' sales as spare parts.

## Excluded restrictions (Art. 5 — severable, verified)

- **Non-compete obligations exceeding 5 years** (verified: "five years"),
  including tacitly renewable beyond that;
- post-term non-competes (max 1 year, premises-bound, indispensable for
  know-how protection);
- bans on selling competing brands within selective distribution;
- **wide retail parity clauses** imposed by online intermediation services
  (across-platform parity) — excluded since 2022.

## Withdrawal and disapplication (Arts. 6–7, verified structure)

Commission or NCA may withdraw the benefit in an individual case; the
Commission may by regulation disapply the VBER to networks of parallel
restraints covering > 50 % of a market.
