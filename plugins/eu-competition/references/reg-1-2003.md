## System (Arts. 1–3, verified)

- **Art. 1**: Arts. 101 and 102 TFEU are **directly applicable** — including
  Art. 101(3); no prior notification/authorisation (the "legal exception"
  system replacing Regulation 17/62).
- **Art. 2 — burden of proof**: on the party/authority alleging the
  infringement; the undertaking claiming Art. 101(3) bears the burden for its
  conditions.
- **Art. 3**: NCAs and national courts applying national competition law to
  conduct affecting trade between Member States **must also apply
  Arts. 101/102**; national law may not prohibit agreements that Art. 101
  permits (stricter national unilateral-conduct rules allowed).

## Decisions (Arts. 7–10, verified structure)

| Art. | Decision |
|---|---|
| 7 | Finding and termination of infringement (behavioural or structural remedies — structural only where no equally effective behavioural remedy) |
| 8 | Interim measures (urgency, prima facie infringement) |
| 9 | **Commitment decisions** — binding commitments, no infringement finding; reopening if facts change, commitments breached, or decision based on incomplete/misleading information |
| 10 | Finding of inapplicability (Union public interest) |

## Powers of investigation (Arts. 17–22, verified structure)

- **Art. 17**: sector inquiries.
- **Art. 18**: requests for information — simple request or **decision**
  (incorrect/misleading replies sanctionable).
- **Art. 19**: power to take statements (interviews, with consent).
- **Art. 20**: **inspections** of undertakings — enter premises, examine and
  copy books/records, seal premises, ask on-the-spot questions; by simple
  authorisation or by **decision** (which must be tolerated); national court
  authorisation for coercion per national law.
- **Art. 21**: inspection of **other premises** (incl. directors' homes) where
  reasonable suspicion that books are kept there — prior national judicial
  authorisation required.
- **Art. 22**: investigations by NCAs on the Commission's behalf.

## Sanctions (Arts. 23–24, verified figures)

| Provision | Amount |
|---|---|
| Art. 23(2) — substantive infringements | Fines up to **10 % of total turnover** in the preceding business year (per undertaking) |
| Art. 23(1) — procedural (incorrect info, broken seals…) | Up to **1 % of total turnover** |
| Art. 24 — periodic penalty payments | Up to **5 % of average daily turnover** per day of non-compliance |

Fine-setting method: `references/fines-guidelines-2006.md`. Fines are not
criminal-law in nature (Art. 23(5), verified).

## Cooperation and coherence (Arts. 11–16, verified structure)

European Competition Network: case-allocation and information exchange
(Arts. 11–12), suspension/termination where one authority deals with the case
(Art. 13), Advisory Committee (Art. 14), cooperation with national courts
(Art. 15 — amicus curiae), **uniform application (Art. 16)**: national courts
cannot rule counter to a Commission decision on the same agreement.

## Other key provisions (verified structure)

- **Art. 25**: limitation — 5 years (3 years for procedural infringements),
  interrupted by investigative acts; absolute cap 10 years.
- **Art. 27**: hearings, access to file, statement of objections — rights of
  defence; Hearing Officer practice.
- **Art. 29**: withdrawal of a block exemption in an individual case.
