## Union dimension — turnover thresholds (Art. 1, verified)

**Art. 1(2)** — a concentration has a Union dimension where:
- combined aggregate **worldwide** turnover of all undertakings concerned
  > **EUR 5 000 million**, **and**
- aggregate **Union-wide** turnover of **each of at least two** undertakings
  concerned > **EUR 250 million** —
unless each of the undertakings concerned achieves more than **two thirds of
its aggregate Union-wide turnover within one and the same Member State**
(the "two-thirds rule", verified).

**Art. 1(3)** — alternative thresholds: worldwide > **EUR 2 500 million**;
in **each of at least three Member States** combined turnover >
**EUR 100 million**; in each of those three, **each of at least two**
undertakings > **EUR 25 million**; and Union-wide turnover of each of at
least two > **EUR 100 million** — same two-thirds exception.

Turnover calculation: Art. 5 (groups, financial institutions, insurance).

## Concentration (Art. 3, verified structure)

Merger of previously independent undertakings, or **acquisition of control**
(decisive influence, sole or joint, by rights/contracts/other means); a
**full-function joint venture** (lasting autonomous economic entity) is a
concentration — coordination aspects assessed under Art. 101 criteria
(Art. 2(4)).

## Substantive test (Art. 2, verified)

A concentration which would **"significantly impede effective competition in
the common market or in a substantial part of it"** (SIEC), in particular by
creating or strengthening a dominant position, shall be declared incompatible.
Appraisal factors: market structure, actual/potential competition, buyer
power, entry barriers, supply/demand trends, efficiencies. Market definition:
`references/market-definition-notice.md`.

## Procedure and deadlines (Arts. 4, 6–8, 10, verified figures)

| Stage | Deadline (working days) |
|---|---|
| Phase I decision (Art. 6) | **25** from notification |
| Phase I with commitments or referral request | **35** |
| Phase II (Art. 8) | **90** from initiation |
| Phase II where commitments offered | **105** |
| Phase II extension (complexity, at parties' request/consent) | up to **+20** (total max **125**) — the 65-working-day figure is the Art. 9 post-referral marker `[EUR-Lex — verify the exact extension arithmetic against Art. 10]` |

- **Art. 4(1)**: prior notification; **Art. 7 standstill** — no implementation
  before clearance (derogations possible, public bids exception).
- Failure to decide in time = deemed compatible (Art. 10(6), verified).

## Referrals (Arts. 4(4)–(5), 9, 22, verified structure)

- **Art. 4(4)/(5)**: pre-notification referrals at the parties' initiative
  (to a Member State / to the Commission — 4(5) requires no veto from any
  competent Member State within 15 working days).
- **Art. 9**: post-notification referral to a Member State (distinct market).
- **Art. 22**: Member State(s) referral to the Commission — scope after
  *Illumina/Grail* case law `[CJEU — verify Curia reference]`.

## Sanctions (Art. 14, verified)

Fines up to **10 % of aggregate turnover** for implementing before clearance
(gun-jumping), proceeding despite prohibition, or breach of conditions; up to
**1 %** for incorrect/misleading information in notifications.
