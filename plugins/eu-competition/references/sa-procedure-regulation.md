## Notified aid (Chapter II, verified deadlines)

- **Notification duty** (Art. 2) and **standstill** (Art. 3): aid shall not be
  put into effect before authorisation (Art. 108(3) TFEU).
- **Preliminary examination** (Art. 4): decision within **2 months of
  complete notification** — no aid / no objections (compatible) / open the
  **formal investigation procedure**. Silence after 2 months: aid deemed
  authorised after Member State notice (Art. 4(6)).
- **Formal investigation** (Arts. 6–9): interested parties' comments;
  closure by positive, conditional or **negative decision**; the Commission
  shall "as far as possible" decide within **18 months from opening**
  (Art. 9(6), verified); beyond it, the Member State may request a decision
  within 2 months.

## Unlawful aid (Chapter III, verified)

- Aid put into effect in breach of standstill: information injunction,
  **suspension injunction**, and exceptionally a **recovery injunction**
  (Art. 13).
- **Recovery decision** (Art. 16): recovery from the beneficiary **with
  interest**, unless contrary to a general principle of Union law; national
  procedures must allow immediate and effective execution.
- **Limitation period: 10 years** for recovery of unlawful aid (Art. 17,
  verified), interrupted by any Commission or Member State action concerning
  the aid; aid beyond it is deemed **existing aid**.

## Other procedures (verified structure)

| Chapter | Subject |
|---|---|
| IV | Misuse of aid — formal investigation may be opened directly |
| V | **Existing aid** — constant review, appropriate measures proposals; no recovery, only prospective modification |
| VI | Interested parties — complaints via mandatory complaint form; information of outcome |
| VII | **Sector inquiries** into aid instruments across Member States |
| VIII | Monitoring — annual reports, **on-site monitoring visits** |
