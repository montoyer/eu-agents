# GBER & De Minimis — Thresholds and Aid Intensities (2024)

Curated static reference for the `eu-competition` skills (`gber-screener`,
`state-aid-review`, `lawyer-state-aid`). These figures change when the Commission
amends the block exemption regulations — read them from this file; **do not
generate them from training data**.

## De Minimis ceilings (Reg. 2023/2831)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| General de minimis ceiling | **EUR 300,000** per single undertaking over any 3-year period |
| Reference period | Rolling 3 years (changed from 3 fiscal years under 1407/2013) |
| Road freight transport (separate sub-ceiling removed) | EUR 300,000 (the former EUR 100,000 sub-ceiling was abolished in 2023/2831) |
| SGEI de minimis (Reg. 2023/2832) | EUR 750,000 over 3 years |
| Central register obligation | Member States must record de minimis aid in a national/EU register from 1 Jan 2026 |

## Key aid intensities (Art. 7 ceilings)

| Category | Large | Medium (+bonus) | Small (+bonus) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SME investment (Art. 17) | — | 10% | 20% |
| R&D — industrial research (Art. 25) | 50% | +10% | +20% |
| R&D — experimental development (Art. 25) | 25% | +10% | +20% |
| Training (Art. 31) | 50% | +10% | +20% (+10% disadvantaged) |
| Energy efficiency (Art. 38) | 30% | +10% | +20% |

Regional aid intensities follow the approved regional aid map under
Art. 107(3)(a)/(c) — read the applicable map; do not generalise.

## Transparency / publication threshold

- Individual aid award publication in the Transparency Award Module: **EUR 100,000**
  (Art. 9 GBER). Reduced to EUR 10,000 in the agriculture/fisheries primary
  production sectors.
